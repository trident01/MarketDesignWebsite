Fellowship Opportunity

Postdoctoral Fellowship

One-year postdoctoral fellowship supported by the Stanford Center for Computational Market Design

Duration
One year, with possibility of renewal
Sponsor
Stanford Center for Computational Market Design
Location
Stanford University, Stanford, CA

About the Fellowship

The Stanford Center for Computational Market Design invites applications for a postdoctoral research fellowship. This fellowship is designed to support outstanding early-career researchers who wish to pursue interdisciplinary work at the intersection of algorithm design, economics, machine learning, and operations research.

Fellows will have the opportunity to collaborate with our world-class faculty and engage with a vibrant research community dedicated to tackling timely challenges in modern market design.

Areas of Interest

  • Algorithm design and computational complexity in market settings
  • Economic theory, mechanism design, and auction theory
  • Machine learning for market analysis and prediction
  • Operations research and optimization for market platforms
  • Empirical market design in domains such as education, health, labor, and transportation
  • AI and market design, including reinforcement learning and data-driven approaches

How to Apply

Required Application Materials:

  • Curriculum vitae
  • Three letters of reference
  • A brief statement
  • Cover letter including which faculty at Stanford you would like to interact with

Send application material to Itai Ashlagi (iashlagi@stanford.edu) and Amin Saberi (saberi@stanford.edu).
